THE MAN BEHIND THE CHAIR

MEET
TRAVIS WILSON.

BARBER.
AIR FORCE VETERAN.

After more than 20 years of dedicated service in the United States Air Force, Travis retired in 2012—but retirement didn't slow him down. Ready for a new challenge, and wanting to continue to serve his community, he mastered a new craft. After co-founding Girlcrush Salon with Tawnya in 2016, Travis went on to establish GC's Man Cave Barbershop, blending old-school craftsmanship with modern style, military pride, and a whole lot of personality to La Vernia, Texas.

"Here, it's about more than looking good when you leave. It's about feeling welcome while you're here."
